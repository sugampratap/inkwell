Inkwell is a notebook for people who think with a pen. The ink stays under the nib, the pages feel like paper, and everything you write stays editable. Move it, restyle it or erase it whenever you like.

## Why I built Inkwell

I always wanted a perfect note-taking app, but none of the apps out there met my needs. I was even paying for a subscription and still wasn't happy with the writing experience.

So, after weighing up what was possible, I started building my own. Claude Opus 5.5 sped up the process a hundredfold.

I've also long wanted to give something back to the open-source community. It has given me so many great apps that I've relied on for years.

I hope you'll like Inkwell. If there's anything you'd like to see, let me know.

## Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your notes are plain files in a folder you choose, never uploaded anywhere. They aren't locked in a database, so you can back them up, sync them or move them with any tool you like.

<sub>The optional document scanner uses Google Play services.</sub>

## Near-zero latency

Ink is drawn straight to the screen, outside the normal frame queue, with a predicted stroke just ahead of the nib, so the line stays under your S Pen. The pens, pencil and lasso stay smooth on long, fast strokes, and the cost stays flat however long the stroke gets.

## A state-of-the-art writing experience

- **Six pens:** fountain, ballpoint, brush, calligraphy, dashed, and a **textured pencil** whose graphite darkens as you press.
- **Highlighter and study tape:** hold to straighten a highlight; tape hides answers until you peel it back.
- **Palm rejection:** rest your hand on the screen and write.
- **Shape snapping:** draw, hold, and it snaps to a clean line, arrow, circle or box. Drag a line's ends anywhere.
- **Sharp at any zoom,** with zoom lock on the toolbar.

## Unparalleled customisation

- **Toolbar:** rearrange it into sections, show or hide any tool, float it or dock it on any edge, and pick its size.
- **Looks:** Light, Dark and true-black OLED; sharp, rounded or soft corners; any accent colour, dual tone or Material You.
- **Selection bar:** choose which buttons appear for handwriting, pictures, sticky notes, text and tables.
- **Pages:** 20+ templates (Cornell, planners, music staves, isometric and more) with their own colours and spacing, per page or for the whole notebook.
- **Pen box:** save your favourite pens and highlighters as presets.

## More than handwriting

- **Notebooks and an infinite canvas:** pages when you want structure, endless space when you don't.
- **PDFs:** import any PDF and write all over it. Highlight and comment on text, search it, widen the margins for side notes, and filter the colours for night reading.
- **Typed notes:** text boxes, sticky notes, tables, checklists, markdown, LaTeX and highlighted code.
- **Images:** insert, crop and arrange them, or scan a document straight in.
- **Audio:** record while you write, then play back with your notes replaying in sync, from 0.5× to 2×.
- **Share:** as a sharp vector PDF with bookmarks, an editable PDF, images or an Inkwell note.
- **Organise:** a library with favourites, tags, search, trash and split view.

Inkwell is built on top of xnotes, an open-source handwriting app, and adds its own design, tools and features on that foundation.

## Install

Download the latest APK from [Releases](https://github.com/sugampratap/inkwell/releases/latest) and open it on your tablet. Inkwell needs Android 8.0 or later on a 64-bit ARM device, and is tuned on the Galaxy Tab S8 with the S Pen.

## Build from source

Needs JDK 17, NDK 27.0.12077973 and CMake 3.22.1.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/sugampratap/inkwell.git
cd inkwell
./gradlew assembleDebug
```
